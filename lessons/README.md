# Lessons: Elastic Security Timelines

Eight lessons for a 60-minute demo, taught with the tell-show-tell method. All data is synthetic.

## Goal

Teach Timelines, and show the value of a Workflow that collates timeline notes into a weekly situation report.

The customer problem: analyst notes between investigations are hard to share and manage.

## Tell-show-tell format

Every lesson uses the same sections, so each maps cleanly to 1 to 3 Google Slides later.

| Section | Purpose | Slide hint |
|---|---|---|
| Learning objective | One sentence: what the audience can do afterwards | Title slide text |
| Tell | One Timelines concept, in plain product terms, before the demo | 1 slide |
| Show: what to look for | What the audience should notice in the demo. No click steps | 1 slide, or screenshot |
| Tell: the recap | The one idea to keep, and the bridge to the next lesson | 1 slide |

Click steps, preparation, risks and fallbacks live in the runbook, not in the lessons.

Rule of thumb: Tell teaches one Timelines point. Show proves it live. The recap repeats the one idea they should keep.
